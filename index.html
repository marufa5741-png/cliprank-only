Frequently Asked Questions

What does ClipRank do?

ClipRank analyses a long-form video transcript, ranks candidate moments, and generates a hook, title, caption, hashtags and suggested timestamp for each clip.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The Clip Analyzer on this page works without an account.

Can I analyse someone else’s video?

Only if you own the content or have written permission to repurpose it. ClipRank does not download, host or redistribute third-party videos.

Which platforms are supported?

Outputs are formatted for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, with 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 options.

Is ClipRank free?

The Clip Analyzer on this page is free. Future hosted features, if introduced, will be listed on a pricing page.

Does a high score guarantee views?

No. Scores rank candidates within one source video. Performance depends on topic, timing, distribution and audience.
